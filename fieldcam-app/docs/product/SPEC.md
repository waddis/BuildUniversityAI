# fieldcam.app Product Specification

## Vision
fieldcam.app helps field teams capture, organize, and report jobsite documentation from the phone to the office.

## Target Users
- Public adjusters
- Insurance inspectors
- Contractors
- Restoration teams
- Property managers

## Core Modules
1. **Auth & Tenancy** — Multi-tenant company accounts with role-based access
2. **Projects** — Job/project management with customer and property details
3. **Media** — Photo/video upload, gallery, metadata, thumbnails
4. **Collaboration** — Notes, comments, mentions, activity feed
5. **Workflow** — Tasks, checklists, templates, proof photos
6. **Markup** — Photo annotations (arrows, text, shapes)
7. **Reporting** — PDF report builder with selected media and notes
8. **Sharing** — Secure external links for reports and projects

## Roles
- Owner, Admin, Manager, Field User, Viewer

## Industry Fields
- claim_number, policyholder_name, inspection_type
- damage_category, room_or_elevation, loss_date
- site_contact, carrier_reference
